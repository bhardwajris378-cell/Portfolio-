Systems inquiry

I look at the whole ecosystem before I touch the interface. The surface problem is rarely the real one, so I widen the lens, ask, map, and only then design.

01

Widen the lens

Move outward from the problem to the user, the community and the wider ecosystem around it.

02

Inquire

Ask open questions through interviews, field visits and observation instead of assuming.

03

Map the system

Stakeholder maps, journey maps, service blueprints and causal loops show how the parts depend on each other.

04

Find leverage points

Locate where a small change creates a significant outcome, and name the gap precisely.

05

Design and test

Translate insight into a design response, then test and refine it with people.
