01

Happy Faces
Foundation

Research project · Systems design

A systems-design study of Happy Faces Foundation's women artisans in Karnali, looking for small changes that lead to significant outcomes. I mapped demographics, stakeholders and resources, ran a 9-question interview study and built 3 personas. Causal loops and a SWOT found four leverage points: carbon-neutral shipping, on-site quality control, childcare, and local role models.

02

Supermarket: Next-Gen Application

Research project · UX research & app design

A UX research and app design project on how customers find their way around supermarkets, following the Double Diamond. I interviewed 28 customers and staff, visited about 20 supermarkets in Gandhinagar and ran a 50-response survey, then built 2 personas, journey maps and a service blueprint. The answer: an AR companion app that guides shoppers from entry to the nearest free checkout.

03

Woodo: From Channapatna, the City of Toys

Research project · Toy design

A toy design project for Woodo by PlayShifu, rooted in Channapatna, the city of toys. Toys are made for play, amusement and learning, and they support creativity, cognitive development, social skills and physical coordination in children. The approach runs from research (secondary research and insights), through analysis and synthesis (systems modelling, user needs and gaps, potential interventions), to a solution: product and design decisions, a design brief and a prototype.

05

The Place Where I Feel Like Myself

Qualitative research · Synthesis

A 19-person interview series asking where people feel most like themselves, and what that place is really giving them. People named a bedroom, a terrace, a temple or a mountain, but rarely separated the place from what it gave them. I synthesised six needs: safe space, escape, self-expression, memory, connection and emotional regulation.

UI design · Mobile app
